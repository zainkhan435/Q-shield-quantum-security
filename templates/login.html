SCIENTIFIC FOUNDATIONS

How Q-SHIELD Detects Protocol Threats

The platform replaces computational hardness assumptions with information-theoretic quantum physics and rigorous statistical mechanics.

01

Quantum-State Simulation

Synthesizes single-qubit signature states |ψ⟩ on the interactive 3D Bloch sphere, tracking exact pure and mixed density matrices.

02

Bell-State Entanglement

Distributes maximally entangled EPR pairs |Φ⁺⟩ across channels, forming a statistically correlated quantum bridge.

03

Quantum Teleportation

Executes Bennett's 3-qubit teleportation protocol to transmit signature states without physical qubit traversal.

04

Projective Measurements

Verifies signature parity in mutually unbiased Pauli bases (Z, X, Y). Wavefunction collapse introduces detectable errors on inspection.

05

Quantum Bit Error Rate

Empirically measures orthogonal measurement outcomes against the theoretical 11.0% cutoff, separating noise from eavesdropping.

06

Statistical Hypothesis Testing

Applies binomial Z-scores to measurement distributions (Z ≥ 3.0σ), ensuring 99.73% confidence statistically verified threat alerts.

CYBERSECURITY ATTACK SURFACE

Detectable Cyber Threat Vectors

Five critical attack scenarios simulated, analyzed, and audited against deterministic verification criteria.

VECTOR 01 Parity Collapse

Signature Forgery

Adversaries alter the quantum payload state (|ψ'⟩ ≠ |ψ⟩). Projective verification yields ~100% QBER and immediate rejection.

VECTOR 02 Entanglement Mismatch

Signer Impersonation

Adversaries without valid pre-shared entanglement produce uncorrelated random distributions, flagged by multiparty verification.

VECTOR 03 Nonce Collision

Replay Attacks

Re-transmitted classical bits detected by cryptographic single-use UUIDv4 nonce ledger, statistically confirmed at 3σ confidence.

VECTOR 04 Wavefunction Collapse

Intercept & Resend (Eve)

Eve taps the quantum channel, collapsing the Bell pair and inducing 25-50% QBER, exceeding the 11.0% cutoff.

VECTOR 05 Depolarizing Noise

Channel Manipulation

Environmental decoherence or fiber tampering forcing errors beyond safe margins classified via Z-score elevation (Z ≥ 3.0σ).
