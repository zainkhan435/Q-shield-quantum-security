Teleportation-Based Quantum Digital Signatures

Alice shares an entangled Bell state |Φ⁺⟩ with Bob and Charlie. By measuring her unknown signature state |ψ⟩ jointly with her Bell qubit, the signature collapses and classical bits (c₀, c₁) are transmitted to allow exact Pauli reconstruction.
